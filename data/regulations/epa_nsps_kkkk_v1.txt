ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY
40 CFR Part 60
[EPA-HQ-OAR-2023-0105]
Standards of Performance for Stationary Combustion Turbines

PROPOSED RULE
(Issued September 10, 2024)
Comments must be received by November 15, 2024.

### Section 1. Applicability and Affected Facilities
The provisions of this subpart are proposed to apply to all stationary combustion turbines with a heat input at peak load equal to or greater than 50 MMBtu/h that commenced construction, modification, or reconstruction after the proposal date.

### Section 2. Emission Limits for Nitrogen Oxides (NOx)
Owners and operators of simple-cycle natural gas-fired combustion turbines serving electric generation stations shall maintain emissions of nitrogen oxides (NOx) not to exceed 15 ppmvd at 15 percent O2 during baseload operation.

### Section 3. Monitoring and Reporting Requirements
The owner or operator must install, calibrate, maintain, and operate a continuous emission monitoring system (CEMS) for NOx. Semi-annual compliance summary reports must be submitted to the Administrator within 45 days after the end of each reporting period.
